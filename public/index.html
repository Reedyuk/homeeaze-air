Built for UK & EU Homes

US advice doesn't apply here. Here's why.

Most air quality products and advice come from the US, where homes have central HVAC. UK and EU homes work completely differently — and that changes everything.

🏠 US home — with central HVAC
  • Central air continuously circulates through every room
  • Filtration and purification covers the whole house at once
  • One sensor in one room gives a broadly representative picture
  • Pollutants mix and level out across the home
🏡 UK/EU home — no central HVAC
  • No central air circulation — each room is effectively isolated
  • Doors closed at night trap CO₂ and other pollutants
  • Post-2016 airtight new builds have minimal passive ventilation
  • Room-by-room ventilation quality varies wildly across the same home
  • CO₂ and PM2.5 behave very differently in sealed rooms
💡

In a typical UK home, your sealed bedroom can hit 2,000 ppm CO₂ while your living room reads 600 ppm — at the same time, in the same house. A single hallway sensor gives you neither reading.

Sensor Transparency

What we're measuring, and how accurately.

We think you deserve to know exactly what's inside. Here's the sensor platform we're designing around, and the accuracy targets we're holding ourselves to.

Pollutant Sensor class Accuracy target Range
CO₂ NDIR (e.g. Sensirion SCD40-class) ±50 ppm + 3% of reading 400–5,000 ppm
PM2.5 Laser particle counter ±10 μg/m³ < 100 μg/m³ 0–500 μg/m³
VOC MOX with on-chip compensation Index 0–500 (relative) 0–1,000 ppb equiv.
Temperature Integrated MEMS ±0.3°C -10–60°C
Humidity Capacitive polymer ±2% RH 0–95% RH

Sensor class and specs represent design targets for prototype units. Final production hardware will be confirmed and published before any pilot ships.

🔧

Factory + field calibration

Sensors are factory-calibrated before shipping. Ongoing drift compensation is handled via firmware — no user action, no subscription required.

⏱️

30-second measurement cycle

Readings are taken every 30 seconds. Stored locally at full resolution. The app shows rolling averages to reduce noise without hiding real events.

📂

Raw data always accessible

You can export your raw timestamped readings at any time via the API or CSV export. We don't round, smooth, or hide inconvenient data from you.

Why Now

UK homes have an air quality crisis.

This isn't a new problem — but awareness has never been higher, and the solutions have never been this accessible.

🏗️

UK new-build ventilation failures

Post-2016 building regulations demand airtight homes for energy efficiency — but ventilation standards haven't kept pace. Thousands of new UK homes trap pollutants inside with no way out.

😴

CO₂ is silently ruining your sleep

Research shows CO₂ above 1,000 ppm significantly impairs sleep quality. In a sealed bedroom with two people, levels routinely exceed 2,000 ppm by morning — without anyone realising.

🍳

PM2.5 from cooking is underestimated

A 20-minute cooking session can push kitchen PM2.5 to 10× the WHO safe limit. These fine particles travel through your home and persist for hours — long after the smell is gone.

🦠

Post-COVID awareness is at an all-time high

COVID-19 fundamentally changed how people think about indoor air. Ventilation is now a mainstream concern — and people are actively looking for tools to take control of what they breathe.
